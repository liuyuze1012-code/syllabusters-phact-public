# Practice pattern library: sources

Every source below was actually opened and read for the v1 pattern library (`kb/practice/`), on **2026-11-04**. The cards are written in PHACT's own words. Each card has at most one short quote (under 15 words), and every card cites its sources. Where a card's design is PHACT's own synthesis rather than taken from a source, the card says so.

**Licence rule for this library:** the cards are summaries in PHACT's own words, with citations, so they do not reproduce any source. Nothing is copied wholesale, including from CC-licensed sources. Several sources are CC **BY-NC** or **BY-NC-SA**. If PHACT is ever sold commercially, these sources can still be summarized and cited, but none of their text or figures can be reproduced. That restriction applies to the AIAS papers, Searle and Waterloo.

## Notes for reviewers
- **AIAS level names differ by source.** The revised paper uses "AI-Assisted Planning" (L2) and "AI-Assisted Task Completion" (L3). The website now uses "AI Planning" and "AI Collaboration". The `aias-levels` card gives both.
- **The arXiv abstract page** for 2412.09029 shows an author count that does not match the PDF. The PDF names three authors (Perkins, Roe, Furze) and thanks MacVaugh. The cards follow the PDF.
- **AIAS pilot results** (fewer AI misconduct cases, +5.9% attainment, +33.3% pass rates) are described by the authors themselves as preliminary and partly confounded by a policy change. The cards say so.
- **Failure modes not taken from a source** are labelled PHACT synthesis on each card. These are common patterns, not measured rates, so a domain expert should check them.
